LD Nova AI Secretary Agent

Your AI Secretary. Your Business, Always Moving.

Let an intelligent assistant help manage everyday business operations, respond to customer inquiries, coordinate appointments, and organize incoming leads, so your team can focus on higher-value work.

WhatsApp customer communication

Connect the agent to your business WhatsApp account through a supported integration. It assists customers around the clock, answers frequently asked questions using approved business information, collects customer details and classifies inquiries.

Appointment management

Help customers request or schedule appointments. Connect to your approved calendar or scheduling system and send confirmations and reminders when the relevant integrations are configured.

Customer reminders

Send supported reminders through WhatsApp and email using configurable message templates, respecting customer consent, messaging policies and communication preferences.

Google Sheets & lead management

Record and organize customer information in Google Sheets, capture potential customers from supported channels, track inquiry status and support follow-up workflows.

Human handoff

Transfer a conversation to a person when the agent cannot resolve a request, when a customer asks for one, or when escalation is required. Transfer mechanisms depend on the configured messaging platform and workflow.

Business efficiency assessment

We analyze your business processes, identify repetitive work and bottlenecks, and recommend suitable automation tools: less manual work, better-organized customer data, more consistent follow-up and better visibility into your workflows.

Implementation scope, platform compatibility, usage limits and third-party requirements are confirmed in the service agreement. Not every capability is included in every configuration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the AI Secretary Agent do?

It helps answer customer inquiries, organize leads, coordinate appointments and send supported reminders, escalating to a person when needed.

What is the difference between the $499 and the $130?

$499 is a one-time implementation fee to set up your assistant. $130 per month is the recurring service. Exact scope and deliverables are confirmed in the service agreement.

Are third-party platform costs included?

Not necessarily. Subscriptions or usage charges from external platforms, such as messaging providers, may be separate, if applicable.

Can a person take over a conversation?

Yes. Human handoff workflows can be configured, depending on your messaging platform and workflow.

Which businesses is it for?

Entrepreneurs, small businesses, growing companies and established businesses.
